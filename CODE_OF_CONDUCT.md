# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in MedPaper Assistant welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- showing empathy, respect, and professional disagreement;
- giving specific, evidence-based feedback and accepting responsibility for mistakes;
- respecting research participants, private data, authorship, licenses, and disclosure rules;
- focusing critique on contributions and methods rather than people;
- disclosing relevant conflicts and avoiding fabricated credentials or evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption, plagiarism, research misconduct, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, community spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where possible, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

For non-sensitive concerns, open an issue with only the minimum information needed to request maintainer contact. Do not publish personal or confidential details. Until the project publishes a dedicated private conduct address, use [GitHub's report-abuse process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) for confidential or urgent conduct reports involving GitHub content or accounts. Security vulnerabilities must instead use the [private security advisory form](https://github.com/u9401066/med-paper-assistant/security/advisories/new).

Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are involved in a report. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited. Knowingly false reports may themselves be treated as misconduct.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may apply these steps according to impact and pattern:

1. **Correction** — private clarification and a request to correct the behavior.
2. **Warning** — documented conditions and consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction** — a time-limited pause in participation or contact.
4. **Permanent restriction** — removal from project spaces for sustained or severe harm.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
